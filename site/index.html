What it does

One prompt, every AI

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, DeepSeek. Ask once and compare the answers side by side, without typing the same thing five times or handing anyone an API key.

See where your waiting goes

Which AI is slowest, how often you tab away mid-answer, and what it adds up to over a week. Exportable, and yours.

It tells you when something is wrong

Signed out, hit a usage limit, or showing a verification check — you find out straight away, rather than discovering an hour later that a prompt went nowhere.

Privacy

Your prompts and timings stay in your browser. There is no account, no analytics, and no telemetry.

  • It never reads the AI's replies. It watches only whether an answer is still arriving, so it can time it.
  • It never sees your password. You sign in yourself, in your own browser.
  • Your prompt is held only while it is being delivered to the AIs you chose, then deleted. Keeping a history is off by default.
  • Two network requests exist, and neither carries your data: a public file telling the extension where each site's send button is, and — only if you ever buy something — a one-off licence check. Nothing is sent when you send a prompt.

How it behaves

whileAI works only inside sessions you have already signed into. It types at human pace, one prompt at a time per AI, and it does not bypass anything: no CAPTCHA solving, no working around a usage limit, no hidden sessions, no pretending to be a different browser. When a site shows a verification check or says you have hit a limit, whileAI stops and tells you — that is the whole of its response.

Something not working?

These AI sites change their pages often, and when one does, whileAI can stop recognising it. Fixes ship without waiting for a store review, usually within hours — but only if someone says something. There is a Tell me what's broken or missing link at the bottom of the extension's panel, or you can email me directly.
